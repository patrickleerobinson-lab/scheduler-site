Plan together

Less scheduling. More playing.

Keep important dates and session details in one shared calendar. Scheduler was created for tabletop roleplaying communities and is designed to be useful for other community gatherings, too.

Release features

Everything your group needs to get organized.

01 / CALENDAR

Shared event calendar

See scheduled activities and open event details from a community calendar.

02 / EVENTS

Detailed event planning

Add titles, times, descriptions, notes, categories, colors, and reference links.

03 / REPEAT

Recurring sessions

Organize regular games and recurring events, with controls for individual sessions and series changes.

04 / INVITE

Invitations

Invite community members to scheduled activities and manage who is expected.

05 / RSVP

Attendance responses

Track Going, Maybe, and Can't Attend responses to help organizers plan ahead.

06 / UPDATES

Root notifications

Keep invited members informed of event invitations and relevant cancellations or changes.

07 / CONTROL

Organizer permissions

Event management permissions help communities control who can organize their calendar.

08 / FLEXIBILITY

More than game night

Use Scheduler for campaign sessions, group meetings, community gatherings, and other events.

Feature availability may vary by community configuration. Additional capabilities will continue to evolve after launch.

Getting started

Built to live inside Root.

Scheduler is a Root community app. Once its public listing is approved and published, community administrators can follow the Root Store listing to make it available to their members. Until then, public installation is not yet available.
