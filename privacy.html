Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Scheduler is a community calendar application developed for tabletop roleplaying games and related activities within the Root platform. This policy explains the information the application uses, why it uses that information, and the choices available to community members.

1. Scope and relationship with Root

Scheduler provides a shared calendar for community events and gaming sessions. It operates within Root and relies on Root services for identity, community information, communication, and application storage. Root's own privacy policies and terms govern its independent processing of account and platform information. This policy addresses information used by Scheduler itself.

2. Information used by Scheduler

Scheduler may access Root user and community identifiers and assigned community roles to identify the community in which it operates and determine whether a member is authorized to manage calendar events. Members do not need to create a separate Scheduler account.

Calendar entries contain information entered by organizers, including event titles, descriptions, dates, start and end times, and event identifiers. Event records may also contain an identifier associated with the person who created the event. Users should not include passwords, confidential contact information, financial details, or other sensitive information in shared event descriptions.

3. How information is used

Scheduler uses this information to display community calendars, maintain event records between sessions, process requests to create or modify events, and check permissions before changes are made. Scheduler does not use event information for targeted advertising or behavioral profiling and does not sell personal information.

4. Storage and service providers

Scheduler uses application storage and communication services provided through Root. Calendar entries remain available between sessions through those services. Scheduler does not require an external calendar account or independent registration. Root controls aspects of the underlying infrastructure, including any platform backups and retention procedures, which are outside the developer's direct control.

5. Community visibility and permissions

Calendar events are intended to be visible to members of the community where Scheduler is installed. Event titles, descriptions, and times should therefore be treated as shared community information, not private messages. The application is designed to restrict event creation, editing, and deletion to community administrators or other authorized organizers. These role-based controls are under development and testing before wider release.

6. Sharing of information

Scheduler does not sell, rent, or provide calendar information to advertising networks. Information is processed through Root services as necessary for the application to operate. Scheduler does not currently require an independent third-party analytics or marketing service. If future features materially change these practices, this policy will be updated.

7. Retention and deletion

Calendar entries are maintained as needed to provide the shared calendar. Authorized organizers can delete individual events through the application. Members seeking correction or removal of event details should contact their community administrator or the developer using the address below. Removing an event from the active calendar does not guarantee immediate deletion of any copies retained in Root-controlled backups or operational systems. Account-level or platform-level deletion requests should be directed to Root.

8. Security

Scheduler uses Root application services for community identity, data storage, and communications. Its design includes permission checks for event management actions. No online service can guarantee absolute security, and members should avoid putting sensitive personal information into calendar entries. Suspected unauthorized changes or privacy issues may be reported using the contact information below.

9. Children's privacy

Scheduler is intended for use in Root communities subject to Root's applicable age and membership requirements. It is not designed to independently solicit personal information from children. Members and organizers should avoid entering identifying or sensitive information about minors in shared events.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised as Scheduler develops or its information practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when revisions are published. The latest version will remain available at this address.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy, Scheduler's handling of information, or requests relating to the application can be sent to patrickleerobinson@gmail.com. For changes to a specific community calendar entry, members may also contact their community administrator. Questions about Root accounts or Root-controlled information should be directed to Root Support.
